# Testing the lamp screens

- [When to run these checks](#when-to-run-these-checks)
- [Setup](#setup)
- [Checks](#checks)

Tapping a Brightness or Temperature dial's part of the touch strip shows one screen per lamp ([#48]), where the dial controls that lamp alone ([#20]). The unit tests check against a fake Stream Deck which lamp comes next, when a dial goes back to its main screen, which changes redraw a lamp screen, that a turn, a push and a hold ask for the lamp on screen, a turn's ticks for the lamp they were turned on, and that the power of a lamp that's off is drawn in dark grey, and in white again once it's on. They check that, with the blink on for the dial ([#144]), a tap blinks the lamp it shows once its screen is drawn, the way back to the main screen blinks nothing, a tap during a blink waits for it to end, and a dial that chose otherwise on its own page doesn't blink while the others do. They check that a blink, on a hold or a tap, lasts as long as the plugin settings say ([#171]), and that each cell shows what the plugin settings choose for it, or nothing, as its options say: an identification's parts, a name or its fallback, a part with no text left out, a trimmed identification, each count and format, a unit, an icon, and the power's label, color and weight for each state, and that a cell's options for one item don't change another's ([#177]). They check that every text takes the Primary text style by default, the power's "OFF" the Secondary one, each cell the style chosen for it, and each power state the style chosen for it, or its own color and weight at the Primary style's size ([#153]). They check against emulated lamps that a blink, a toggle and a step reach that lamp alone. The integration tests check against emulated lamps that a lamp screen shows what its lamp reports and follows what other actions change, that what it sends fits `layouts/lamp.json`, that each dial turns and toggles its lamp alone until a turn on a main screen brings the lamps back in sync, that a dial taken off the Deck is forgotten, so the lamps changing no longer redraw it, that once a settings page turns the blink on, a tap on any dial blinks the lamp it shows, and that a change to the cells reaches every dial on a lamp screen. The browser tests check that each dial's page shows the dial's own checkbox, and the plugin settings every dial's, unchecked by default and indeterminate when the dials differ, what a click on each saves to the plugin's global settings, and that the two stay in step on a dial's page, and the plugin settings' list for each cell: its default, its choices, and what it saves, and each cell's editor: that it takes the plugin settings' place and gives it back, offers the options the plugin takes at their defaults, greys out the counter's format for a lamp's number alone, and a power state's color and weight unless its font style is Custom, saves a change by cell and by item, and that Reset takes them away, and the text styles' controls: their defaults, their choices, what they save, and that each style's Reset saves its defaults, keeping the other style. The checks below cover what they take for granted: that Stream Deck delivers the taps and holds, switches to the lamp layout and back, and draws it legibly, with the slots each cell hides out of sight, that it keeps the global settings and passes them on to the plugin, and that the lamps report what they show.

## When to run these checks

- After a change to the lamp screens, to their layout in `layouts/lamp.json`, to how the plugin reads the lamps or picks the ones an operation acts on, or to the plugin's global settings.
- After a Stream Deck update.

## Setup

Plug in two Beams, turned on at different brightness and temperature, using their own buttons if needed. Place a Brightness dial and a Temperature dial on the Deck, and a Power key. Build the branch and restart the plugin:

```shell
npm run build
npx streamdeck restart com.daczarne.litra
```

In VS Code, the `Build and restart` task runs both.

## Checks

1. **The screen is readable.** Tap the Brightness dial's strip. It shows the first lamp: "Beam - " followed by the last 4 characters of its serial number, with "1 of 2" at the right, on the first row, the thermometer icon with the kelvin and "ON" at the right on the second, and the sun icon with the % and the lumens on the third. Every value reads clearly from where you sit. Share a photo of the strip.
2. **Each lamp in turn.** Tap again: the strip shows the other lamp's label, "2 of 2" and its values. Each label is the lamp's model and the end of its serial number, as the plugin settings list it, in the same order ([`plugin_settings.md`][plugin-settings]). Tap again: the strip goes back to the brightness with its bar.
3. **Holding blinks the lamp on screen.** With both lamps at 0%, on a lamp screen, hold the strip: only that lamp fades to dark, clearly enough to tell even with the other one on, and comes back at the same brightness and temperature, and the strip stays on its screen. Turn that lamp off with its own button, tap through to its screen again, and hold: it lights up for a moment and goes off again. On the main screen, holding the strip does nothing.
4. **A lamp screen stays.** Tap to a lamp screen and leave the dial alone for a minute: the strip still shows the lamp screen. Only tapping past the last lamp goes back to the main screen.
5. **Turning moves the lamp on screen.** On the Brightness dial, tap to "1 of 2" and turn the dial: only lamp 1's brightness changes, and its screen shows the new %. Tap through both screens and back: the new % is still on "1 of 2". On the Temperature dial, tap to "2 of 2" and turn: only lamp 2's temperature changes. If lamp 1 was off, the brightness turn lights it, as a turn on the main screen does.
6. **Pushing toggles the lamp on screen.** On a lamp screen, push the dial: only that lamp turns off, and the screen shows "OFF" in dark grey, while its other text stays white. Push again: it comes back on at the same brightness and temperature, and the screen shows "ON" in white, like its other text. On the main screen, a push still toggles every lamp.
7. **A quick turn stays with its lamp.** On "1 of 2", turn the dial quickly by a few ticks and tap the strip straight away: only lamp 1 moves, and lamp 2, now on screen, keeps its values.
8. **The main screen brings the lamps back in sync.** With neither "Each lamp steps from its own" setting checked in the plugin settings, the default, and the lamps set apart, tap past the last lamp and turn the Brightness dial one tick: both lamps go to the same brightness. Do the same with the Temperature dial: both go to the same temperature.
9. **Other changes show.** Put the Temperature dial on a lamp screen, then turn the Brightness dial and press the Power key: the Temperature dial's lamp screen shows the new brightness, then the new power state.
10. **The app describes the gestures.** Select the dial in Stream Deck: it lists "Brightness" or "Temperature" for rotating, "Power" for a push and "Lamps" for touching the strip. On a lamp screen, it lists "Brightness" or "Temperature" for rotating, "Power" for a push, "Next lamp" for a touch and "Identify" for a long touch.
11. **Unplugging the other lamp mid-cycle.** Tap to "1 of 2", unplug the other lamp, and tap: the strip goes back to the main screen. Plug it back in.
12. **Unplugging the lamp on screen, then tapping.** Tap to "1 of 2", unplug the lamp on screen, and tap: the strip shows the remaining lamp as "1 of 1". Plug it back in.
13. **Unplugging the lamp on screen, then turning.** Tap to a lamp's screen, unplug that lamp, and turn the dial: the dial's part of the strip flashes red, Stream Deck's alert on a dial, and goes back to the main screen. Plug it back in.
14. **Switching pages.** Tap to a lamp screen, switch to another page of the Deck and back: the dial shows its main screen.
15. **Blinking each lamp as it's shown is off by default.** Select the Brightness dial in Stream Deck: under "Lamp screens", "Blink each lamp as it's shown, on this dial" is unchecked. Press Plugin settings: under "Dials", in "Lamp screens", "Blink" shows "Blink each lamp as it's shown, on every dial", unchecked too. Tap through the lamp screens: no lamp blinks.
16. **Blinking each lamp as it's shown.** Check every dial's in the plugin settings, and tap the Brightness dial's strip: the first lamp's screen shows, then that lamp alone goes off for a moment and comes back as it was. Tap again: the second lamp's screen shows, and it blinks. Tap again: the strip goes back to the main screen, and no lamp blinks.
17. **Every dial's reaches every dial.** Select the Temperature dial: its own checkbox is checked. Tap through its lamp screens: each lamp blinks as it's shown.
18. **A dial can choose otherwise.** On the Temperature dial's page, uncheck its own checkbox. Tap through its lamp screens: no lamp blinks. Tap through the Brightness dial's: each lamp still blinks. Open the plugin settings, on either dial or on a key: every dial's checkbox shows a bar, since the dials differ. Share a screenshot of it.
19. **Every dial's gives every dial the same again.** Click every dial's checkbox, showing its bar: it's checked, and the Temperature dial's own checkbox is checked again, and both dials blink. Click it again: it's unchecked, both dials' own checkboxes are unchecked, and neither dial blinks.
20. **A dial can blink alone.** With every dial's unchecked, check the Brightness dial's own: only the Brightness dial blinks, and every dial's checkbox shows a bar.
21. **A quick tap waits for the blink.** With the Brightness dial blinking, tap its strip twice quickly: the first lamp blinks to the end, before the second lamp's screen shows and it blinks in turn.
22. **The blink outlasts a restart.** With only the Brightness dial blinking, as in check 20, restart the plugin with `npx streamdeck restart com.daczarne.litra`, and tap through both dials' lamp screens: only the Brightness dial's blink. Quit Stream Deck and open it again: still only the Brightness dial's, and its own checkbox is still checked. Click every dial's checkbox until it's unchecked, to leave the default.
23. **The cells show the same items as before, by default.** Press Plugin settings: under "Layout", the lists read "Identification" and "Lamp counter", "Temperature" and "Power", "Brightness %" and "Brightness lm". Tap to a lamp screen: it's laid out as in check 1. Share a screenshot of the plugin settings.
24. **A cell shows what it's given.** With the Brightness dial on a lamp screen, set the top right list to "Temperature": the dial's top row shows the kelvin with the thermometer icon on its right, against the strip's right edge, without tapping. Set the bottom left to "Power": "ON" or "OFF" shows where the % was, without the sun icon, at the left edge. Share a photo of the strip.
25. **None empties a cell.** Set the middle left list to "None": the thermometer icon and the kelvin disappear, and the rest of the screen stays where it was.
26. **Every dial shows the same.** Tap the Temperature dial to a lamp screen: it shows the same cells as the Brightness dial.
27. **The cells outlast a restart.** Restart the plugin and tap to a lamp screen: the cells are still as set. Set every list back to its default, as in check 23.
28. **Configure shows a cell's options.** Press Plugin settings, then the Configure button under the top left list: the plugin settings give way to "Cell" reading "Top left: Identification", "First part" at Model, "Second part" at ID, an unchecked Trim, "Font style" at Primary, and a Reset button. Press Back: the plugin settings show again. Share a screenshot of the editor.
29. **The identification's parts.** Name the first lamp "Studio key light" in the plugin settings. In the top left cell's editor, set the first part to Name and the second to Empty: the first lamp's screen shows "Studio key light", running into the counter, and the second lamp's shows its model and ID, having no name. Check Trim: the name shows as "Studio ke…", clear of the counter. Share a photo of the strip.
30. **The counter's formats.** In the top right cell's editor, try each format: "1/2", "1-2" and "1 2". Set the count to Lamp number: the screen shows "1", then "2", and Format is greyed out. Set it to Count by model: Format can be chosen again, and with two Beams, the screen shows the same as with Count of total lamps.
31. **The icon and the unit.** The middle left cell's editor shows Icon, Unit and Font style, and no icon side. Uncheck Unit: the kelvin shows without " K", with the thermometer still on its left. Uncheck Icon: the kelvin shows at the left edge, where the icon was. Share a photo of the strip without the icon.
32. **The lumens with the sun.** In the bottom right cell's editor, check Icon: the sun shows against the right edge, with the lumens before it.
33. **The power's looks.** In the middle right cell's editor, "On state" and "Off state" each show centered above two rows, Label and Font style, then Color and Font weight, side by side, each with its caption above it. Font style reads Primary for the On state and Secondary for the Off state, and Color and Font weight are greyed out under both. By default, "OFF" reads in dark grey, in the normal weight, against the strip's background. Set the On state's label to "●", its font style to Custom, its color to an orange, and its weight to Bold: Color and Font weight become available, and the power shows a bold orange "●". Set the Off state's label to "○", and turn the lamp off with a push: it shows a dark grey "○". Try each label, and check that "●" and "○" read on the strip. Share a photo of the editor and of each label.
34. **Each item keeps its options.** Set the top left list to Lamp counter, then back to Identification: its editor still shows the parts and Trim set in check 29.
35. **Reset.** In each edited cell's editor, press Reset: its options go back to their defaults, and the lamp screen shows what it did in check 1. Set the first lamp's name back to empty.
36. **The text styles.** In the plugin settings, under "Text styles", "Primary", "Secondary", "Accent" and "Alert" each show Color, Font weight and Font size side by side, with a Reset button under them: white, dark grey and cyan, Normal, and a soft red, Bold, all at 18 px. Share a screenshot.
37. **Primary reaches every text.** With the Brightness dial on a lamp screen, set Primary's color to a yellow and its weight to Bold: every text turns bold yellow, without tapping, and the thermometer and the sun turn yellow with it, as sharp as before, while "OFF" stays dark grey.
38. **The sizes fit.** Set Primary's size to 22 px: every text grows, without being cut off or running into the row above or below. Share a photo. Set it to 16 px: every text still reads from where you sit. Share a photo.
39. **Secondary reaches the power's "OFF".** Turn the lamp on screen off with a push, and set Secondary's color to an orange and its weight to Bold: "OFF" turns bold orange. In the middle right cell's editor, set the Off state's font style to Primary: "OFF" shows like the rest of the text.
40. **A cell picks its style.** With Secondary bold orange, as in check 39, open the top right cell's editor: its Font style reads Primary, under its other options, and offers Secondary, Accent and Alert too. Set it to Secondary: the counter turns bold orange, while the rest of the text stays in Primary. Set it to Accent: the counter turns cyan, and reads on the strip. Set it to Alert: it turns a bold soft red, and reads too. Share a photo of each. Set it back to Primary. Set the middle left cell, the temperature, to Accent: the thermometer turns cyan with the kelvin. Set it back to Primary.
41. **Back to the defaults.** Press Primary's Reset: its controls go back to white, Normal, 18 px, and the lamp screen's text with them, while "OFF" stays bold orange. Press Secondary's Reset: "OFF" goes back to dark grey. Press Reset in the middle right cell's editor: the lamp screen shows what it did in check 1. Restart the plugin: the styles are still at their defaults.
